Family Rafting Trip

Enjoy a fun and relaxing rafting experience designed for families and beginners. Our experienced guides will help you safely explore the river while enjoying beautiful scenery along the way.

Whitewater Adventure

Experience the thrill of whitewater rafting on challenging rapids. This trip is perfect for adventure seekers looking for an adrenaline rush. Our guides will ensure your safety while you enjoy the excitement of navigating the river's twists and turns.

Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging

Duration: 3-4 hours

For experienced rafters seeking the ultimate adrenaline rush, this trip takes you through some of the most challenging rapids in the region. Our expert guides will lead you through the twists and turns of the river, providing an unforgettable adventure.

Difficulty: Advanced

Duration: 5-6 hours
